The Aging World: Old-Age Dependency Ratio, 1990–2024

Elderly people (65+) per 100 working-age adults (15–64). Japan now has 51 elderly dependents per 100 workers — tripling since 1990. Sub-Saharan Africa remains far younger, with fewer than 5 per 100.

Source: World Bank — Age dependency ratio, old (SP.POP.DPND.OL)

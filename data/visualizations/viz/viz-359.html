Share of Population Under 15, by World Region, 1960–2024

The demographic transition in numbers: East Asia & Pacific dropped from 40% to 18% while Sub-Saharan Africa has barely moved below 41%. A high youth share signals rapid population growth ahead; a low share signals aging and potential labor shortages.

Source: World Bank — Population Ages 0–14 (SP.POP.0014.TO.ZS)

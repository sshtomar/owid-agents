Old-Age Dependency Ratio, 1960–2024

Number of people aged 65+ per 100 working-age adults (ages 15–64). Japan leads the world's aging shift — more than 50 retirees per 100 workers — while South Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa remain young.

Source: World Bank — Age Dependency Ratio, Old (% of working-age population) (SP.POP.DPND.OL)

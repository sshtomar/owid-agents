The Early Learning Gap: Pre-Primary Enrollment, ~1990 vs ~2020

Gross enrollment ratio in pre-primary education (can exceed 100% due to age overlaps). Ghana tripled its rate; Iran went from under 10% to 72%. Sub-Saharan countries like Burkina Faso and DR Congo remain below 7% — leaving most young children without early schooling.

Source: World Bank SE.PRE.ENRR · ~1990 vs ~2020

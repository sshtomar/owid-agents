Old-Age Dependency Ratio, 1960–2020

Elderly dependents (age 65+) per 100 working-age people (age 15–64). Japan has the world's most extreme aging burden. South Korea is on a steeper trajectory than Japan was at the same point in time. India, Indonesia, and Ethiopia remain young.

Source: World Bank — Age dependency ratio, old (SP.POP.DPND.OL)

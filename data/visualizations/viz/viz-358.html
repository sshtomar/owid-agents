Old-Age Dependency Ratio, 1960-2024

Elderly (65+) per 100 working-age people (15-64). Japan now has 51 elderly per 100 workers -- the world's most aged society.

Japan accelerated from 8.9 to 50.7 over 64 years. Korea (5.6 → 27.5) is aging even faster in relative terms. Ethiopia (5.1 → 5.6) and Iraq (5.9 → 5.7) remain young societies with barely any change.

Source: World Bank, SP.POP.DPND.OL

Male vs. Female Labor Force Participation, 2023

Each line connects female rate (left) to male rate (right) for one country. The steeper the slope, the wider the gender gap. Afghanistan, Iraq, and Iran show the largest gaps; Burundi shows female rate exceeding male.

Source: World Bank — SL.TLF.CACT.MA.ZS & SL.TLF.CACT.FE.ZS · 138 countries · 2023

Democratic Backsliding, 2000-2025

V-Dem Electoral Democracy Index (0-1) for nine countries. Hungary fell from 0.81 to 0.42 under Orban; Turkey from 0.65 to 0.29 under Erdogan; India from 0.74 to 0.38; Hong Kong collapsed to 0.15 after the 2020 security law. Sri Lanka is a rare counter-trend; Sweden remains the global benchmark.

Source: Our World in Data / V-Dem Electoral Democracy Index (1209753)

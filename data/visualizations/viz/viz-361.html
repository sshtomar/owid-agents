Resource Dependency: Natural Resource Rents as % of GDP, 2000–2021

Total rents from oil, gas, coal, minerals, and forests as a share of GDP. Oil price crashes in 2009 and 2015 are visible across all petrostates; Iraq's extreme exposure reflects its near-total dependence on oil exports.

Source: World Bank — Total Natural Resource Rents, % of GDP (NY.GDP.TOTL.RT.ZS)

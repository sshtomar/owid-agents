DPT Immunization Coverage, 1980–2024

% of children ages 12–23 months immunized against diphtheria, pertussis and tetanus. Bangladesh rose from under 2% in 1985 to 97% by 2009 — one of the fastest vaccination scale-ups ever recorded.

Source: World Bank — Immunization, DPT (% of children ages 12-23 months) (SH.IMM.IDPT)

Breast Cancer 5-Year Survival Rate, 2021

Net survival rate (%) five years after diagnosis. In high-income countries, more than 9 in 10 patients survive. In Sub-Saharan Africa, fewer than 3 in 10 do — a gap driven by late-stage diagnosis and access to treatment.

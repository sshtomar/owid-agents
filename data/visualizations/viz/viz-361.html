Anemia Prevalence Among Women of Reproductive Age, 2000 vs 2022

Percentage of women ages 15–49 with anemia. India remains the most populated high-burden country and has slightly worsened. Afghanistan increased by 13 percentage points. Many West African countries show modest improvement but remain above 40%.

Source: World Bank — Prevalence of anemia among women of reproductive age (SH.ANM.ALLW.ZS)

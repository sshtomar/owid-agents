The Uneven War on Poverty: $3/day Poverty Rates Since 1981

Share of population living below $3.00 a day (2021 PPP). East Asia's poverty rate collapsed from 77% in 1981 to under 2% today, driven largely by China. Sub-Saharan Africa barely moved. South Asia followed East Asia on a lag.

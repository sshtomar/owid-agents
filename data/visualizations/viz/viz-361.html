Fossil Fuel Share of Energy Consumption, 1990 → 2014

% of total energy from fossil fuels. Iceland slashed its share by 17 points (geothermal and hydro). Estonia dropped 24 points after Soviet industrial collapse. China surged as coal-powered growth accelerated. Japan climbed after shutting nuclear plants post-Fukushima.

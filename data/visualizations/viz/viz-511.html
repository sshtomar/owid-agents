Contraceptive Prevalence: From Near-Zero to Majority

Share of married women (ages 15–49) using any modern contraceptive method, comparing ~1990 to latest available year. Many countries in sub-Saharan Africa started below 5% but have since reached 15–57%.

Source: World Bank — Contraceptive Prevalence, Any Modern Method (SP.DYN.CONM.ZS)

The Aging World: Old-Age Dependency Ratio, 1960–2024

Number of people aged 65+ per 100 working-age people (ages 15–64). Japan's ratio has nearly sextupled since 1960, now exceeding 50 — meaning more than one retiree for every two workers.

Source: World Bank — Age Dependency Ratio, Old (SP.POP.DPND.OL)

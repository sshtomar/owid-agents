Natural Gas's Rising Role in Electricity Generation, 1990–2024

Share of electricity produced from natural gas (% of total). Israel switched from near-zero to 70% after developing offshore gas fields. Japan's 2011 spike followed Fukushima nuclear shutdowns. Argentina and Italy rely heavily on gas as a bridge fuel.

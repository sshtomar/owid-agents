Crude Death Rate, 1960-2023

Deaths per 1,000 people across 16 countries. Two forces collide: health progress drives rates down, aging pushes them back up.

Bulgaria (8.1 → 15.7) and Japan (7.6 → 13.0) now have higher death rates than 1960 due to population aging. Bangladesh (21.6 → 5.0) and Ethiopia (23.9 → 6.0) achieved the steepest declines.

Source: World Bank, SP.DYN.CDRT.IN

DPT Immunization Coverage by World Region, 1980–2024

Share of children ages 12–23 months vaccinated against diphtheria, pertussis, and tetanus. Africa and South Asia closed a vast gap with high-income regions over four decades. Dip visible in 2020–21 from COVID-19 disruptions.

Source: World Bank — Immunization, DPT (SH.IMM.IDPT)

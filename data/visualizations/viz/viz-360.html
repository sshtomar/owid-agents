Sex Ratio at Birth, 1960–2024

Male births per female births. The biological baseline is approximately 1.05. Values significantly above this threshold indicate sex-selective practices (abortion or under-registration of female births). China peaked at 1.18 in the 2000s; Azerbaijan and Armenia spiked sharply after the Soviet collapse as ultrasound became widespread.

Source: World Bank — Sex ratio at birth (male births per female births)

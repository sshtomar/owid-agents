Fossil Fuel Energy Consumption (% of Total), 1990–2014

Share of energy consumption derived from coal, oil, and natural gas. Japan surged after the 2011 Fukushima disaster as nuclear capacity shut down. Iceland and Brazil, powered by geothermal and hydro, remain outliers. China deepened its fossil fuel dependence through the 2000s.

Source: World Bank — Fossil fuel energy consumption (% of total) (EG.USE.COMM.FO.ZS)

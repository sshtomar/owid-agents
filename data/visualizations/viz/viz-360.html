Electricity from Natural Gas, 1990-2022

Share of electricity generated from natural gas (% of total), selected countries.

Bahrain (100%) and Algeria (99%) run almost entirely on gas. Japan pivoted sharply to gas after the 2011 Fukushima nuclear shutdown. China and India generate only ~3% of electricity from gas, relying instead on coal.

Source: World Bank, EG.ELC.NGAS.ZS

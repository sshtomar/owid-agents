Battle-Related Deaths by Country, 1989–2024

Estimated annual deaths from organised armed conflict. Ethiopia's Tigray war (2020–2022) and Israel's 2023–2024 conflict produced spikes that dwarf earlier peaks. Afghanistan's war lasted over three decades. Values capped at 35,000 for readability — Ethiopia's 2022 peak reached ~125,000.

Source: World Bank — Battle-Related Deaths (VC.BTL.DETH)

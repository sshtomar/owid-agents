Antiretroviral Therapy Coverage, 2000–2024

Share of people living with HIV who receive antiretroviral treatment (%). In 2000, less than 1% of HIV-positive people in sub-Saharan Africa had access to treatment. By 2024, Eastern and Southern Africa — the region with the world's highest HIV burden — reached 84%.

Source: World Bank — SH.HIV.ARTC.ZS

The Demographic Transition: Youth Population Share (Ages 0–14), 1960–2024

Percentage of the population aged 0–14. Countries go through a demographic transition as fertility falls — Sub-Saharan Africa remains in early stages while East Asia has compressed this shift into decades. South Korea dropped from 41% to 11% in 65 years.

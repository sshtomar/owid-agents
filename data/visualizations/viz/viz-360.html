HIV Incidence Rate, 1990–2024

New HIV infections per 1,000 uninfected people ages 15–49. Eswatini and Botswana peaked above 50 per 1,000 in the mid-1990s — meaning 5% of uninfected adults became infected each year. Sustained prevention and treatment programs have since driven dramatic declines across sub-Saharan Africa.

Source: World Bank — Incidence of HIV, Ages 15–49 (SH.HIV.INCD.ZS) · UNAIDS

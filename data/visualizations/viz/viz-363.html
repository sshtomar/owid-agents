Poverty at $3/Day: Asia's Descent, Africa's Struggle

Share of population living below $3.00 per day (2021 PPP). China, Indonesia, and India have cut rates from over 80% to near zero in three decades. Sub-Saharan African countries have seen far slower progress.

Source: World Bank — Poverty Headcount Ratio at $3/day (SI.POV.DDAY) · 2021 PPP

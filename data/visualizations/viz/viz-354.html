Financial Inclusion: Account Ownership 2011 vs 2021

Share of adults (ages 15+) with an account at a financial institution or mobile-money provider. India went from 35% to 78% in a decade through its Jan Dhan banking push. Gabon, Bolivia, and Ghana made similarly dramatic gains. Rich-country saturation is near-total.

Source: World Bank — FX.OWN.TOTL.ZS (Global Findex Database)

Researchers in R&D per Million People, 1996–2023

Full-time equivalent researchers in R&D per million people. South Korea quintupled its research workforce in 27 years, surpassing Japan. Denmark and Finland are among the world's most research-intensive economies. China is rising fast from a low base.

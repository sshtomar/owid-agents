Population Growth Rate by World Region, 1961–2024

Annual population growth rate (%) by world region. Sub-Saharan Africa remains the fastest-growing region at ~2.4%, while East Asia & Pacific has fallen from over 2.5% in the 1960s to just 0.2% today — one of the sharpest demographic transitions in history.

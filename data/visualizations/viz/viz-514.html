Foreign Direct Investment Inflows, 1990–2024 (% of GDP)

Net FDI inflows as share of GDP, 3-year smoothed. China's dramatic rise and retreat mirrors its growth trajectory; China attracted peak FDI in the 1990s as wages were low, then declined as manufacturing costs rose. Colombia and Ethiopia show how targeted reforms can draw investment to frontier economies.

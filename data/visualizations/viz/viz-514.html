Coal Rents as a Share of GDP, 1980–2021

Government revenues from coal extraction as a percentage of GDP. All major coal economies spiked in 2008 during the commodity boom; all have since declined as renewables displace coal. China's early 1980s peak reflects subsidised domestic coal before market liberalisation.

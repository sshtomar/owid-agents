The Carbon Burden: CO₂ Damage as a Share of National Income, 2021

Adjusted savings: the economic cost of CO₂ emissions as a percentage of Gross National Income. This measure captures how much of a nation's wealth is effectively consumed by carbon damage — fossil-fuel exporters and coal-heavy economies pay the heaviest toll.

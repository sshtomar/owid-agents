Rise of the Megacity: Share of Population in Cities over 1 Million, 1960–2024

China went from 8% to 32% of its population living in megacities since 1960. Japan's share doubled. Congo's Brazzaville now accounts for 65% of the country's total population.

Source: World Bank — Population in Urban Agglomerations of more than 1 Million (% of Total), EN.URB.MCTY.TL.ZS

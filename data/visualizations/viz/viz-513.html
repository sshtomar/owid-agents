Freshwater Withdrawal as % of Internal Resources

Annual freshwater withdrawn for agriculture, industry and domestic use, as a share of total renewable internal freshwater resources. Values above 100% mean a country draws on fossil aquifers, cross-border rivers, or desalination—unsustainable long-term. The 100% threshold marks the boundary of renewable supply.

Source: World Bank — ER.H2O.FWTL.ZS (AQUASTAT/FAO)

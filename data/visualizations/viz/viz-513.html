Youth at Work: Labor Force Participation for Ages 15–24, 1990–2024

Share of 15–24 year-olds who are working or actively seeking work. Nordic and Oceanic economies show high rates; MENA and Mediterranean countries struggle with youth idleness. China has seen a sharp decline as education expands.

Source: World Bank / ILO — SL.TLF.ACTI.1524.ZS (modeled ILO estimate)

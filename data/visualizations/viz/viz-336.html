The Happiness Shift

Self-reported life satisfaction (Cantril ladder, 0-10), 2011 vs 2025. China gained 1.4 points as living standards rose, while Afghanistan collapsed by 2.8 after the 2021 Taliban takeover. The US, Canada, and Australia all slid; Iceland and Germany climbed.

Source: Our World in Data / Gallup World Poll - Cantril Ladder (1210090)

The World's Happiest Countries, 2025

Self-reported life satisfaction (Cantril ladder, 0-10). Finland tops the ranking for the eighth straight year. Mexico breaks into the top 15 ahead of Australia. Afghanistan, at 1.45, has collapsed from 4.26 in 2011.

Source: Our World in Data / Gallup World Poll (Cantril Ladder). Values for 2025 with arrow indicating change since 2011.

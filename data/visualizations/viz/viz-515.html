Girls Surviving to Age 5: Under-5 Female Mortality, 1990 vs. 2022

Deaths per 1,000 female live births before age five. Across three decades, almost every country cut rates dramatically — Bangladesh dropped 80%, Angola 77%. Yet Chad and Guinea still lose nearly 1 in 10 girls before their fifth birthday.

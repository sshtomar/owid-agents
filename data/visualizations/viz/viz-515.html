Tariff Rate on Manufactured Products, Weighted Mean (%)

Average tariff applied to imports of manufactured goods, 1988–2022. India's tariff fell from 71% to 8%; China's from 36% to 3% — reflecting three decades of global trade liberalization.

Source: World Bank — Tariff rate, manufactured products, weighted mean (TM.TAX.MANF.WM.FN.ZS)

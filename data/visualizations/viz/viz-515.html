Income Inequality: The Top 20%'s Share, 1990–2024

Share of national income held by the wealthiest 20% of the population. Latin American countries cluster around 50–65%; European welfare states sit at 35–41%. Most Latin American economies have gradually reduced this gap, but remain far more unequal than Europe.

Source: World Bank — Income Share Held by Highest 20% (SI.DST.05TH.20)

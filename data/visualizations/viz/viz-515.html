Forest Rents as a Share of GDP, 1980–2021

Government revenues from timber and forest extraction as a percentage of GDP. Countries with the highest forest rents are almost all among the world's poorest — forests provide economic income where alternatives are scarce. Congo DRC, Burundi, and Guinea-Bissau remain highly dependent.

Energy Vulnerability: Net Energy Imports, 2000–2022

Net energy imports as a percentage of energy use (100% = fully import-dependent). Denmark transformed from a North Sea oil exporter to an importer as reserves depleted. Germany's dependence rose sharply. China and India grew from near self-sufficiency to significant importers.

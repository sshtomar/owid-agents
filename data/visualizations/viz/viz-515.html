New Business Formation Growth, 2010–2023 (Index: 2010 = 100)

New businesses registered per year, indexed to 2010 = 100. Japan simplified its company registration process, triggering a 5x surge. Chile and China tripled. Germany stagnated. The COVID dip (2020) is visible in most countries, followed by a sharp rebound.

Source: World Bank · IC.BUS.NREG · New businesses registered (number)

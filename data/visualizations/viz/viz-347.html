Neonatal Mortality: Six Decades of Progress

Deaths in the first 28 days of life per 1,000 live births, 1960–2024. Bangladesh cut its rate from 103 to 18; China from 30 to 3. High-income countries plateaued near 1–2 by the 2000s, while Sub-Saharan Africa still lags far behind.

Source: World Bank — SH.DYN.NMRT (Neonatal Mortality Rate)

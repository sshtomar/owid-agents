Trade in Services, % of GDP, 1995–2023

Services trade (banking, tourism, IT, consulting) as a share of the economy. Small open economies cluster at 35–65%; manufacturing giants like Germany, China, and Japan stay below 25%; India's IT-services boom is visible from the late 1990s onward.
